🍎 How to use these resources

These PDFs are designed to be used alongside your copy of The Iron Man by Ted Hughes. Each page combines simplified text with picture symbols to support comprehension. Use the "View Online" button to display on a whiteboard or tablet, or "Download" to print for individual TEACCH workstations. The Red Level indicates complex story language with full sentences and inference questions.
